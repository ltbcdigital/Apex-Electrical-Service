======================================================
TRADESMAN QUOTE ENGINE - SETUP GUIDE
======================================================
Thank you for purchasing the Tradesman Quote Engine! 

This tool is designed to work completely offline on your phone, allowing you to generate professional, branded PDF quotes while standing right in front of the client.

------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO INSTALL & USE
------------------------------------------------------
1. Send the "index.html" file to your smartphone (you can email it to yourself, or use Google Drive/Dropbox).
2. Tap the file on your phone to open it directly in your mobile web browser.
3. Tap the "⚙️ Settings" button at the top right of the screen.
4. Fill in your business name, pick your brand colour, and add your bank details. 
5. Tap "Save Changes". 
6. Save the page as a Bookmark or add it to your browser's favorites for quick access.

Note: Your settings are saved securely to your phone's memory. You only need to enter your business details once!

------------------------------------------------------
SENDING QUOTES TO CLIENTS
------------------------------------------------------
When you are ready to send a quote, tap the "Generate PDF Quote" button at the bottom of the screen. This will open your phone's print menu. Simply select "Save as PDF" or use the "Share" icon to send it directly to your client via WhatsApp or Email.
